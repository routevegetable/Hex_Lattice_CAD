# Hinge Hexagon — Segment Measurements

Source: `Hinge Hexagon.zip` → `hinge_obj/Hinge Hexagon.obj`
(OBJ header: *"generated by ONSHAPE … 1.217 Units = meters"*.)

## ⚠️ Units (important)
The OBJ coordinates are **not millimetres and not metres** — they are Onshape
export units. The header states the scale directly:

> **1 OBJ unit = 1.217 m** (`"1.217 Units = meters"`).

**All user-supplied dimensions are in mm and must be divided by 1217 mm/unit
before use in OBJ space.** For example the hinge distance:

> 496.213 mm ÷ 1217 mm/unit = **0.40774 units** from center.

## Supplied specification (given by the user)
- The structure is one repeating **segment**, tiling **laterally** and **vertically** to form a lattice.
- Each joint between segments is a **hinge of arbitrary angle**, **typically 20°**.
- The segment is **laterally symmetrical**.
- Hinges are located **496.213 mm from the center**.
- Segments **stack at increments of 1320 mm**.
- In the OBJ: **Z axis is vertical**, **X axis is lateral**.

## Coordinate convention (from the OBJ)
- **X** — lateral (segments tile sideways along X)
- **Y** — depth / thickness (thin dimension)
- **Z** — vertical (segments stack along Z)

## Lateral center (from the highest-Z points)
The segment apex sits on its lateral symmetry center, so we take the mean X/Y of
the vertices within 2 mm of max-Z (the renderer does this at load time):

> **Center = (X 0.49621, Y 0.17976) units** (116 apex points; X span 0.4835–0.5089).

## Derived hinge / lattice parameters (OBJ units)
- **Hinge half-span:** 496.213 mm ÷ 1217 = **0.40774 units** from center.
- **Hinge pin X positions** (center ± half-span):
  - Left pin:  0.49621 − 0.40774 = **0.08847**
  - Right pin: 0.49621 + 0.40774 = **0.90395**
  - **Lateral period (pin-to-pin) = 0.81547 units** (= 992.426 mm ÷ 1217) — the step between consecutive segments.
- **Pin Y = 0.17976 units** (the apex Y; pin line runs through the segment's Y mid-plane).
- **Hinge axis orientation:** vertical (parallel to **Z**). A lateral hinge folds the panel about this vertical line, so a chain of segments forms a faceted ring/tube (the "hexagon" cross-section).
- **Typical hinge angle:** 20° (arbitrary per joint).
- **Segment is laterally symmetric** about the center X.

## Placement math used by the renderer (all in OBJ units)
Consecutive segments share a hinge pin: segment *i*'s **right pin** coincides with
segment *i+1*'s **left pin**, and the fold angle θ rotates about the **vertical (Z)**
axis through that shared pin.

```
leftPin  = (0.08847, 0.17976, 0)
rightPin = (0.90395, 0.17976, 0)

S(θ)   = Translate(rightPin) · RotateZ(θ) · Translate(−leftPin)
M[0]   = I
M[i+1] = M[i] · S(θ[i+1])
```

θ = 0 ⇒ S = Translate(rightPin − leftPin) = Translate(0.81547, 0, 0) (a straight row).
Vertical level k adds Translate(0, 0, 1.08464 · k) (= 1320 mm).
